## Overview

Poultry mortality is highly sensitive to temperature and humidity deviations. This system automates climate control inside broiler houses using an ESP32 microcontroller and a Node-RED dashboard for remote management.

## Technical Approach

DHT22 sensors sample temperature and humidity every 5 seconds. Readings are published to a Mosquitto MQTT broker, consumed by Node-RED for visualization and threshold-based actuator commands. Relay modules control exhaust fans and misters.

## Results

- Temperature held within ±1°C across a 7-day continuous test
- Humidity deviation under ±5% RH during peak summer conditions
- Remote monitoring accessible via mobile browser with under 2-second latency
