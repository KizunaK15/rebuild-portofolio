## Overview

Oyster mushroom cultivation requires humidity above 80% RH and CO2 below 1000 ppm during fruiting. Manual checks are unreliable and cause yield loss when conditions drift undetected. This IoT system automates monitoring and actuation.

## Technical Approach

DHT22 measures temperature and humidity every 10 seconds; MQ-135 samples air quality as a CO2 proxy. Readings are pushed to Firebase RTDB. A web dashboard visualizes trends and sends browser notifications when thresholds are breached. A relay-controlled humidifier activates automatically when RH falls below 78%.

## Results

- Humidity deviations eliminated — maintained within ±3% RH of 85% setpoint
- CO2 threshold alerts firing within 10 seconds of breach
- Grower reported consistent mushroom flushes with no manual intervention during 30-day trial
