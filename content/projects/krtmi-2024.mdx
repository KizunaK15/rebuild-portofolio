## Overview

The Indonesian Robot Contest — Thematic Category (KRTMI) is an annual national competition requiring teams to build autonomous robots that complete structured mission tasks within a defined arena. This entry was developed by the POLIREVO (Polines Robotics Evolution) team from Politeknik Negeri Semarang.

## Technical Approach

The robot uses a layered control architecture: an IR sensor array handles line detection and path correction, while ultrasonic sensors provide obstacle awareness. A state machine running on the microcontroller sequences through task phases — approach, pick, transport, release — without human intervention.

## Team Role

As Head of KRTMI Division at POLIREVO (Aug 2024 – Aug 2025), Prima led the research and development process, coordinated a multidisciplinary team across software, electronics, and mechanical subsystems, and managed system integration and testing.

## Results

- Robot completed structured task sequences in competition arena conditions
- State machine architecture enabled clean recovery from sensor noise without manual reset
- Team gained competition experience that fed directly into subsequent IoT and embedded projects
