## Overview

Manual material transport on small production floors is repetitive and error-prone. This line follower transporter robot automates fixed-path delivery using a 5-channel IR sensor array and an ESP32 microcontroller, enabling unattended operation without GPS or complex mapping.

## Technical Approach

Five IR sensors continuously sample the line contrast beneath the robot. The ESP32 runs a discrete PID controller at 20 ms intervals, computing a correction signal that adjusts left and right motor speeds through an L298N dual H-bridge driver. A calibration routine runs at startup, sampling each sensor over the actual surface to normalize for lighting variation.

The robot stops automatically at designated delivery points identified by a wider black marker strip, holds for a configurable dwell time, then continues to the next waypoint.

## Results

- 20 consecutive laps completed on a 5-meter oval track without a single line-loss event
- Payload stop position repeatable within 2 cm of the target marker
- Full-track traversal time: 11.8 seconds average at medium throttle (40% PWM)
