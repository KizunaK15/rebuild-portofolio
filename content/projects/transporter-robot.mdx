## Overview

Standard differential-drive robots cannot translate sideways or rotate about a fixed point, limiting their usability in tight spaces. This omni-wheel transporter addresses that by using three mecanum-style omni wheels arranged at 120° intervals for full holonomic motion.

## Technical Approach

Each wheel is driven by a separate DC motor through an L298N driver. The ESP32 runs an inverse kinematics routine that decomposes a desired velocity vector (Vx, Vy, ω) into per-wheel RPM commands. This enables pure lateral movement, diagonal travel, and spin-in-place with no repositioning required.

## Results

- Full omnidirectional movement at up to 0.8 m/s
- Payload transport of 2 kg demonstrated without wheel slip
- Smooth rotation in place with zero turning radius
