## Overview

Industrial automation often demands precision motion that general-purpose motor drivers cannot provide. The Mitsubishi MR-J2S is a professional AC servo amplifier used in manufacturing lines. This project involved commissioning and controlling the drive for a positioning fixture.

## Technical Approach

The MR-J2S was configured in position control mode using pulse-and-direction input. A microcontroller generates the pulse train at a rate proportional to the desired velocity, with step count determining final position. Tuning involved adjusting the amplifier's internal PID and filter parameters via MR Configurator software.

## Results

- Sub-millimeter positioning repeatability confirmed across 100 successive move cycles
- Settling time under 150 ms for quarter-turn moves at medium acceleration
- System demonstrated stable operation under varying load conditions
