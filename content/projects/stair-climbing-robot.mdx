## Overview

Ground-level mobile robots fail at staircase transitions, limiting their deployment in multi-floor environments. This project developed a wheeled-leg hybrid robot capable of climbing standard 15 cm stairs using a state-machine-controlled gait.

## Technical Approach

Three HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors detect step edges at different heights. When a stair is detected, the Arduino Mega switches from drive mode to a four-phase gait: approach, lift, place, transfer. DC motors provide propulsion; servo motors articulate the leg-wheel hybrid joints.

## Results

- 38 out of 40 stair-climbing attempts successful (95% success rate)
- Maximum negotiated step height: 15 cm at 0.3 m/s approach velocity
- Recovery mechanism handles failed attempts within 2 seconds without manual reset
